# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome contributors with different experience levels, languages and backgrounds.

Do not harass, discriminate, threaten or publish personal information. Do not request another person's account credentials, vehicle identifiers or location. Do not encourage unsafe vehicle tests or unauthorized account/vehicle access.

Focus technical discussions on reproducible evidence, not blame. Respect maintainers' time and the distinction between a community project and manufacturer support.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub contact options. For security issues, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit/remove harmful or sensitive content and restrict participation when needed.
